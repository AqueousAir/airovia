Data Centers

AIROVIA supports data center developers and operators with atmospheric water facilities designed for validation, selected support-side applications and campus resilience planning.

Campus Water Resilience

Support-side water infrastructure for high-demand campuses.

AIROVIA is positioned as a dedicated atmospheric water facility layer for validation, resilience planning and selected non-critical support-side water applications.

Validation firstSupport-side applicationsCampus-level planning

Responsible Scope

AIROVIA does not replace the data-center cooling system.

Final integration into mission-critical infrastructure requires engineering review, redundancy analysis, water-quality approval and operational validation. The facility is evaluated as a complementary water-production and resilience layer, not as a substitute for thermal engineering.

Deployment Structure

Facility and production-line planning.
