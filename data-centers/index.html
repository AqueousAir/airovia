The Infrastructure Equation

Water is becoming part of the data center infrastructure equation.

As data centers expand to support cloud, AI, and digital infrastructure growth, water availability is becoming a more important factor in site selection, permitting, cooling strategy, sustainability reporting, and community acceptance.

Even where operators reduce direct cooling water consumption through dry, hybrid, or closed-loop systems, developers still face water-risk questions around facility operations, backup resilience, local infrastructure pressure, and long-term sustainability commitments.

AIROVIA is designed to support this gap by providing localized atmospheric water capacity beside the data center site.

01

Municipal Water Dependency

Reduce pressure on local water networks by generating water directly at or near the data center site.

02

Permitting & Community Pressure

Strengthen the project’s water-resilience narrative when engaging authorities, communities, and infrastructure stakeholders.

03

AI Campus Expansion

Support phased data center growth with modular water-generation infrastructure that can scale with the site.

Responsible Technical Positioning

Engineered with clear boundaries.

AIROVIA’s value is strongest when positioned accurately. It is a water-resilience system, not a universal replacement for all water sources or cooling systems.

AIROVIA is designed to:

  • Reduce municipal water dependency
  • Support non-potable technical water
  • Support cooling make-up water after validation
  • Provide emergency water reserve capacity
  • Strengthen ESG and permitting narratives
  • Scale from one dome to multi-dome infrastructure

AIROVIA should not be presented as:

  • A direct replacement for the data center cooling system
  • A guaranteed replacement for all municipal water
  • A universal solution for every climate
  • A mission-critical cooling component without redundancy
  • A potable water system without local certification

First Deployment Path

Recommended first step: one AIROVIA Dome.
