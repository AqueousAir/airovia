AIROVIA Air House

Signature architecture for visible infrastructure.

The Air House combines industrial atmospheric water production with a recognizable architectural enclosure for sites where visibility, identity and public engagement matter.

The Air House is not required for deployment. The same process architecture can be integrated into conventional industrial buildings, modular plants or custom facilities.
