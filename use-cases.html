AIROVIA Applications

AIROVIA applications are evaluated through water challenge, facility format, integration points, engineering considerations and project pathway.

01

Data Centers

Campus water resilience, selected makeup water, emergency reserve and non-potable site applications.

Format
Pilot production line or campus atmospheric water facility.
Integration
Cooling makeup buffer tanks, BMS/DCIM interfaces, storage, treatment and external utilities.
Engineering
Redundancy, water quality approval, energy availability, operator validation.
02

Industrial Facilities

Operational sites need resilient support water where municipal, trucked or remote supply is constrained.

Format
Standalone facility, integrated facility or modular production lines.
Integration
Process-support water, cleaning, sanitary demand, cooling makeup and storage.
Engineering
Required water quality, uptime, maintenance access and energy profile.
03

Government Water Resilience

Public agencies need diversified localized supply for resilience, pilots and remote communities.

Format
Purpose-built public facility, Air House, modular facility or distributed network.
Integration
Municipal resilience plans, emergency reserves, public infrastructure and innovation districts.
Engineering
Procurement, regulation, community interface, validation and lifecycle governance.
04

Remote Infrastructure

Remote sites face water logistics, transport cost, storage exposure and intermittent access.

Format
Modular facility, integrated utility building or custom off-grid plant.
Integration
Camps, border facilities, islands, research stations and infrastructure corridors.
Engineering
Energy availability, service intervals, climate envelope and logistics.
05

Agriculture and Controlled-Environment Farming

Water quality and local supply can matter most in controlled-environment farming.

Format
Integrated facility or modular plant beside greenhouse, hydroponic or nursery operations.
Integration
Irrigation blending, nutrient systems, storage, treatment and farm utilities.
Engineering
Economic fit, crop value, water quality and energy demand.
06

Hospitality and Destination Developments

Remote resorts and landmark developments need visible, resilient utility strategies.

Format
Air House, custom architectural facility or integrated utility plant.
Integration
Guest-facing infrastructure, irrigation, cleaning, emergency reserve and sustainability campuses.
Engineering
Visual integration, permitting, water classification and operational reliability.
07

Emergency and Strategic Reserve

Programs may need localized production capacity for disruption planning.

Format
Modular or distributed facilities with storage and controlled operation.
Integration
Reserve tanks, emergency distribution and public-sector command structures.
Engineering
Validation, stock management, energy backup and water quality control.
08

New Cities and Infrastructure Corridors

Large developments need utility options during early phases and long-term expansion.

Format
Distributed network, purpose-built facility or phased modular plant.
Integration
District utilities, construction water, operations support and strategic reserves.
Engineering
Demand growth, multi-site governance, civil works and energy planning.
